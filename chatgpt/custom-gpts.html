Custom GPTs: build your first in 20 min.

Custom GPTs are ChatGPT's most underused power feature. Every Plus subscriber can build their own — a purpose-built assistant with custom instructions, conversation starters, knowledge files, and even tool integrations. Most users have never made one. This lesson walks 3 real builds: a sales follow-up GPT, a meeting prep GPT, and an executive summary GPT.

The mental model

Custom GPTs are reusable instructions you build once.

Instead of re-prompting Claude with the same setup every time, you bake the setup into a GPT. Open the GPT, type a short query, get high-quality output that matches the patterns you defined. The 20-minute investment compounds across every future use.

Workflow 01 Build a Sales Follow-Up GPT

1

GPT: "Sales Follow-Up Pro"

A GPT that drafts follow-up emails using the specific-friction pattern and your tone.

The prompt that works

Setup walkthroughChatGPT → Explore GPTs → Create → Name: 'Sales Follow-Up Pro' → Instructions: 'You draft sales follow-up emails. Always ask first: what happened in the prior interaction, what specific detail or friction can I reference, what's the ask. Then draft. Under 80 words, friendly but not desperate, no clichés.' → Conversation starters: 'Draft a follow-up to a ghosted prospect' / 'Help me ask for a renewal'

Best use cases

  • B2B sales reps
  • Account managers
  • Anyone doing customer-facing outreach
  • Founders writing customer emails
Don't put real customer names or deal details in the GPT instructions — they're stored. Keep instructions about process; keep specifics in the conversation.
Time savings: Re-explaining the email pattern: gone forever.

Workflow 02 Build a Meeting Prep GPT

2

GPT: "Meeting Prep"

A GPT that helps you walk into any meeting prepared. Tell it who/what, get a brief.

The prompt that works

Setup walkthroughCreate → 'Meeting Prep' → Instructions: 'When given a meeting topic and attendees, produce: (1) 3-bullet context summary, (2) the 3 most likely outcomes to push for, (3) the 2 most likely objections to prepare for, (4) one good opening question.' → Starters: 'Help me prep for a 1:1' / 'I have a customer meeting in 30 min'

Best use cases

  • Anyone with back-to-back meetings
  • Sales reps prepping for calls
  • Managers prepping for 1:1s and reviews
  • Founders prepping for investor or partner meetings
The GPT can't know specifics about your company or contacts unless you tell it in the prompt. Keep a doc of context to paste in.
Time savings: 10 min of meeting prep → 2 min.

Workflow 03 Build an Executive Summary GPT

3

GPT: "Exec Summary"

A GPT that turns long content into ruthless executive summaries that respect a CFO's attention.

The prompt that works

Setup walkthroughCreate → 'Exec Summary' → Instructions: 'When given any content, produce: (1) one-sentence TLDR, (2) three bullet points of what matters, (3) one bullet of what to do about it. No fluff, no "this comprehensive report explores" garbage. Direct, executive voice.' → Starters: 'Summarize this report' / 'Compress this thread'

Best use cases

  • Anyone who has to forward info to time-poor executives
  • Investor updates
  • Internal team briefings
  • Long-meeting recap summaries
Test the output against a 30-second CEO read. If they couldn't act on it without reading more, the summary failed.
Time savings: Re-drafting summaries to fit attention spans: gone.

Final challenge: build the one GPT that pays off

Pick the workflow you do most often in ChatGPT. Build a Custom GPT for it in 20 minutes. Use it for the next week instead of regular ChatGPT. Most users find the quality jump immediate.
