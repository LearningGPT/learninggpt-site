Which AI for which job?

There's no universal best AI — there's the right one for the task in front of you. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, and Grok are each shaped for different work.

You'll make the call on real tasks, see the right pick and why, and walk away with a 10-second decision tree. About 8 minutes.

01 · "Which AI is best?" is the wrong question

Asking which AI is best is like asking which kitchen knife is best. A chef's knife chops vegetables; a bread knife slices bread; a paring knife peels an apple. One knife handles most jobs — but you'll struggle on the rest.

Match the AI to the work, not the other way around. Power users keep 2–3 AIs open and switch by task. It feels like friction up front; it saves 10× the friction of fighting the wrong tool.

The 10-second decision tree
Current, cited facts? → Perplexity (or a search-enabled mode)
Inside Microsoft 365 / Google Workspace? → Copilot / Gemini
Long-form writing or careful reasoning? → Claude
A reusable custom assistant? → ChatGPT GPTs / Claude Projects
Live news with attitude? → Grok
None of the above? → ChatGPT (the default)

04 · The three mismatches that waste time

Most wasted AI time comes from three avoidable mismatches: using a chat AI for current facts, using Copilot for general writing outside M365, and forcing one AI to do everything because switching feels annoying.
