How this is calculated & assumptions

Pay-per-use = (your message volume × estimated tokens per message) × each model's per-token price. We assume per message — Short ≈ 250 in / 350 out tokens, Medium ≈ 800 in / 1,200 out, Long ≈ 2,500 in / 3,500 out — across ~30 days/month. Real usage varies; treat this as a ballpark, not a bill. Subscriptions are the standard individual plans. Prices as of June 2026 — always check the provider's current pricing. OpenClaw is self-hosted (free software): your cost is whichever model's API you plug in, plus your own hosting/compute — so it tracks the pay-per-use numbers above for the model you choose.
