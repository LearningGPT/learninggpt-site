What AI actually does on the job

Real tasks, with the exact words to type. Copy these, swap in your details, and go.

🧾

Write quotes & estimates

Turn a few rough notes into a clean, itemized estimate a customer can read and trust — in under a minute.

Try thisI'm a [your trade]. Write a clear, itemized estimate for [the job]. Materials: [list]. Labor: [hours] at [$/hr]. Add a friendly one-line intro and a note that the quote is good for 30 days. Keep it professional but plain.
📖

Check code & spec questions

Get a fast, plain-English read on a code or spec question so you know what to look up — then confirm it the right way.

Try thisI'm an electrician in [state]. In simple terms, what does the code generally say about [the situation]? Explain what it means in practice and what I should double-check. Remind me to confirm with my local inspector.
💬

Handle customer messages

The awkward texts — running late, the job got bigger, chasing a payment — written for you, friendly and clear.

Try thisWrite a short, friendly text telling a customer the repair is bigger than I quoted and why, with the new price of [$], so they don't feel blindsided. Offer to call if they have questions.
🔍

Think through a diagnosis

Snap a photo or describe the symptom and get the most likely causes, ranked — a second opinion before you dig in.

Try thisHere's a photo of [the unit/panel/fixture]. The symptom is [what's happening]. List the most likely causes, most common first, and what I should check first. (You're the expert — this is just a starting point.)
⭐

Reviews & simple marketing

Reply to Google reviews, write a quick seasonal post, or draft a simple flyer — without staring at a blank screen.

Try thisWrite three short, friendly replies I can use for Google reviews — one for a great review, one for an okay one, one for an unhappy customer. Keep them human, not corporate.
📋

Knock out paperwork

Change orders, invoices, proposals, job notes — turn your shorthand into clean documents in seconds.

Try thisTurn these notes into a clean, professional change order with a clear description, the reason for the change, and the price difference: [your notes].
One honest rule: AI is fast, but it's sometimes confidently wrong — especially on code, measurements, and safety. You're the expert. Use it to draft, speed up, and get a second opinion — then verify anything that matters with your own knowledge, the manufacturer, or your inspector. We teach you exactly when to trust it and when not to.
