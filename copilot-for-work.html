Outlook — tame your inbox

Triage what needs a reply, then draft responses that use the actual context of the thread — not generic filler.

Try in CopilotSummarize this email thread in 3 bullets, tell me what's being asked of me, and draft a reply that agrees to [the ask] and proposes [a time]. Match the sender's tone and keep it under 90 words.
📊

Excel — analysis without the formulas

Clean messy data, build the right formula, and surface what actually happened — in plain English.

Try in CopilotLook at this sheet. Tell me the top 3 things that changed vs last month, build a column that flags rows over [threshold], and explain each formula you use so I can trust the numbers.
👥

Teams — never miss a meeting again

Catch up on a call you skipped, pull out who-owns-what, and turn a recording into clean notes.

Try in CopilotI missed this meeting. Give me a 5-bullet summary, a list of decisions made, and every action item with who owns it and the due date if mentioned.
📝

Word — from blank page to draft

First drafts, rewrites, and tightening — so you start from something instead of nothing.

Try in CopilotDraft a one-page project update for [audience] covering progress, risks, and next steps. Lead with the bottom line. Professional but plain — no buzzwords like "synergy" or "leverage."
