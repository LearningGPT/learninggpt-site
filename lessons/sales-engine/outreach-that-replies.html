Outreach that actually gets replies.

A great hook still needs a message around it. This lesson is the prompt patterns for cold emails, follow-ups, and LinkedIn messages that feel personal and get responses — personalized on real research, never generic.

The mental model

A reply-getting message is one specific opener, one clear value line, one easy ask — and nothing else.

Most outreach fails by doing too much: a paragraph about you, three features, two asks. The structure that works is short and rigid — open on their specific detail, state the one outcome you offer, make a single low-friction ask. AI is great at this once you give it the research and the shape.

The reframeWrite for the reply, not the sale. The only job of a first message is to start a conversation. Ask for a 15-minute call or a yes/no, never the whole deal.

Step 02 · The outreach prompt

Hand AI the research and the shape — it writes the message, you keep the judgment:

Cold email promptWrite a cold email to [prospect]. Open with this specific detail: [hook from your research]. State one outcome we deliver for companies like theirs: [outcome]. Make one low-friction ask: a 15-minute call. Under 90 words, plain and human, no hype, no "just circling back." Give me 2 subject-line options.
Personalize on truth, not tokens. "Hi [FirstName], love what you're doing at [Company]" is fake personalization — everyone sees through it. Real personalization references something specific you actually found. Generic-at-scale is just spam with a merge field.
