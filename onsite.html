Before the session

The discovery calls are the part that makes it work.

Two to three weeks out, we spend twenty minutes with each department lead. What does your team spend Monday morning on? What report takes a day and shouldn't? What do people already try and give up on?

That is the difference between a class your people sit through and a session where somebody's actual quarterly report gets built in the room. It takes an hour of your leads' time and it changes everything about the day.

Also before we start

A permissions review, available

Copilot surfaces whatever a person already has access to. If old sharing settings are wide open, training people to search well is the fastest way to find that out the hard way. We can check first, or your admin can.

All you need on the day is a room or a meeting link, laptops signed in to your own accounts, and licenses already assigned. Nobody should be installing anything the week before.

What you keep

Yours, permanently.

  • A prompt library for each department — written from their own work, not a generic list
  • Session recordings — for people who missed it, and for everyone you hire next year
  • Everyone's Monday commitment — one specific thing per person, written down
Afterward

We don't leave the same afternoon.

Most trainers hand over a deck and go. The weeks after the session are when a habit either forms or quietly doesn't, so that's where the rest of the work is: regular practice prompts, a standing channel for questions, and a report your leadership can actually read.

How long we stay is scoped with you. These tools change monthly, and keeping pace with them is our job, not your staff's.

Can't get everyone in one room?

Everything above runs over Teams for teams spread across sites, with sessions split into cohorts small enough that people still ask questions. The discovery calls, the prompt library and the follow-through work exactly the same way.
