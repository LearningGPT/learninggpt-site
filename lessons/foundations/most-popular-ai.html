The 10 most popular AI tools, by who actually uses them.

"Most popular" gets thrown around a lot. Here it means something specific: monthly active users — real people coming back, not hype or benchmark wins. As of mid-2026, here's the honest leaderboard, what each tool is genuinely best at, and how to read the numbers without being fooled by them.

How to read these numbers

A leaderboard is not a recommendation. The biggest tool isn't automatically the right one for your task — popularity reflects distribution (who's bundled into Windows, Google, or X) as much as quality. Use this list to know the landscape, not to pick your tool.

Three things skew every ranking you'll see:

  • Web vs. app vs. weekly. ChatGPT's mobile app alone is cited far higher than its "MAU" figure, and OpenAI reports weekly users in the hundreds of millions. Different denominator, different headline.
  • Embedded assistants. Meta AI and Copilot reach people inside other apps, so their "users" aren't comparable to a standalone site like Perplexity.
  • Category mixing. Midjourney (images) and Character.AI (companions) aren't competing with ChatGPT for the same job — they're popular in different lanes.

Frequently asked

Most popular AI — your questions, answered

What is the most popular AI tool in 2026?
ChatGPT, by a wide margin — roughly 300M+ monthly active users and hundreds of millions weekly. Gemini is a distant second, then Copilot and Claude.
What are the 10 most popular AI tools?
By monthly active users in 2026: ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Claude, DeepSeek, Perplexity, Grok, Midjourney (top image generator), Meta AI, and Character.AI. Rankings shift monthly and depend on how users are counted.
Is ChatGPT still the most used AI?
Yes — it remains several times larger than the next competitor across web and mobile. Gemini has grown fast through Google's distribution but hasn't closed the gap.
What's the best free AI tool?
It depends on the job: ChatGPT or Claude for writing and reasoning, Perplexity for cited research, Gemini inside Google, Copilot inside Microsoft 365 — all have strong free tiers. The fastest way to choose is to run the same prompt across a few in the Playground.
How are these rankings measured?
Mostly by monthly active users, but figures vary by source and method — web vs. mobile, monthly vs. weekly, and whether assistants embedded in other apps (like Meta AI) are counted. Treat them as approximate and directional.
