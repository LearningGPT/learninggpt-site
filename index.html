Four steps, and you know what each one costs you in time.

  1. STEP 1 · 15 MIN

    A scoping call

    What your people do, what they already pay for, and whether a day is even the right answer. You get a written quote after this — travel and materials included, no expense invoices later.

  2. STEP 2 · 2–3 WEEKS OUT

    Discovery with your leads

    A short call with each department head. This is the step that turns a generic class into training on the work in front of them.

  3. STEP 3 · THE EVENT

    In your room

    Half a day to three days, on a date you have already set. Hands on keyboards, not slides.

  4. STEP 4 · ONGOING

    Training that continues

    Regular practice, a standing channel for questions, and a report your leadership can read. Most trainers hand over a deck and leave the same afternoon. We scope how long we stay with you, and it is never the same day.
