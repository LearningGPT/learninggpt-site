Claude Fable 5: what happened — and what to use instead.

Fable 5 was Anthropic's headline new model for about 72 hours. It launched on June 9, 2026, and was switched off on June 12 under a US government export-control order — for everyone, everywhere. If you came here looking for it, here's the full story, whether it's coming back, and the Claude model to use in its place.

01 The short version

Claude Fable 5 and Claude Mythos 5 are currently unavailable to all users worldwide. Anthropic disabled them on June 12, 2026 to comply with a US Commerce Department directive. There is no announced restoration date. Everything else in the Claude lineup — Opus, Sonnet, and Haiku — is unaffected and still available. If you were using (or hoping to use) Fable 5, switch to Claude Opus, Anthropic's most capable generally available model.

In one line

Fable 5 is off, your account and data are fine, and Opus is the model to use now. The rest of this lesson is the why and the how.

02 The timeline

June 9, 2026 — Launch
Anthropic releases Claude Fable 5, the first publicly available model in its new top-tier "Mythos" class — positioned above the Opus line as its most capable model yet.
June 12, 2026 — The directive
The US Commerce Department, in a letter from Secretary Howard Lutnick to Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei, directs the company to suspend access to Fable 5 and Mythos 5 for all foreign nationals, anywhere in the world — including those working inside the US.
June 12, 2026 — Global shutdown
Because the restriction reaches foreign nationals everywhere, Anthropic disables both models globally for every user rather than attempt to gate access nationality-by-nationality.

03 Why it happened

The order followed reports of a "jailbreak" — a method of bypassing the models' safety guardrails that, in the government's view, could turn them into unrestricted cyber tools. Anthropic says it reviewed a demonstration of the specific technique and found it surfaced only a small number of previously known, minor vulnerabilities.

Anthropic is complying with the legal directive, but has publicly disagreed that a narrow potential jailbreak should justify recalling a commercial model already deployed to hundreds of millions of people. The practical upshot for you is the same either way: the models are off until that changes.

This is a fast-moving legal and policy situation. Whether — and when — Fable 5 returns depends on the government directive, not on a product roadmap. Treat any "it's back" claim with skepticism unless it comes from Anthropic directly.

04 What to use instead

Map whatever you wanted Fable 5 for onto the model that's actually available:

  • Claude Opus — the most capable model you can use today. Reach for it for the hardest reasoning, long-form writing, complex coding, and analysis. This is the direct stand-in for Fable 5.
  • Claude Sonnet — the balanced everyday workhorse: fast and far cheaper, strong enough for the large majority of real work.
  • Claude Haiku — the fastest, cheapest tier for high-volume, lightweight tasks.

For nearly everything people were excited about with Fable 5, Opus is the answer — and unlike Fable 5, it isn't going anywhere. If you want help choosing between the tiers, see what Claude costs and which AI for which job.

Frequently asked

Fable 5 — your questions, answered

Is Claude Fable 5 still available?
No. Anthropic disabled Fable 5 and Mythos 5 for all users worldwide on June 12, 2026, three days after Fable 5 launched, to comply with a US Commerce Department directive. There's no public restoration date.
Why was Fable 5 removed?
A US Commerce Department letter (from Secretary Howard Lutnick) directed Anthropic to suspend access for all foreign nationals worldwide, after reports of a jailbreak that could bypass the models' guardrails. Because the order reached foreign nationals everywhere, Anthropic disabled the models globally. Anthropic complied while publicly disagreeing that a narrow jailbreak justified the recall.
Does this affect Opus, Sonnet, or Haiku?
No. Only Fable 5 and Mythos 5 are suspended. Opus, Sonnet, and Haiku remain fully available.
What should I use instead?
Use Claude Opus — Anthropic's most capable generally available model — as the direct replacement. Drop to Sonnet for everyday speed and cost, and Haiku for the fastest, cheapest tasks.
What's the difference between Fable 5 and Mythos 5?
Mythos is the name of Anthropic's new top-tier model class, above Opus. Fable 5 was the first publicly available model in it. Both were covered by the same directive and disabled together.
Was my data affected?
The directive concerned access to the models, not user data. Your chats and account are intact — just continue your work on an available model like Opus. As always, keep regulated data out of any AI unless it's been cleared.
