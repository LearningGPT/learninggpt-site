Lesson 09 · Gemini Mastery Pro+ ~13 min 4 surfaces

Slides, Meet, advanced: the rest of Workspace.

Everything else Gemini does beyond Gmail, Docs, and Sheets. Four more surfaces — Slides for decks, Meet for live notes, mobile-native captures, and Calendar/Tasks planning — each with its own win. We'll start with the one that turns a four-hour job into a draft you refine.

The mental model

Gemini lives on more than two apps.

Most people stop at Gmail and Docs. But Slides, Meet, mobile, and Calendar each compound on top of what you already know — and the skill is matching the surface to the moment. First, watch the one that feels like magic.

Do it · Slides from a brief

Describe the deck. Get a draft.

Stop fighting templates. Give Gemini a brief and it drafts the whole deck — structure, layouts, and all. Hit generate.

📊 Gemini in Slides
Brief: 10-slide deck on Q3 results for the leadership team — title, agenda, 3 key points (one slide each), a data slide, Q&A, contact. Clean, professional.
Gemini drafts the structure and layout for every slide.
A full draft deck in seconds. Structure, layout suggestions, even the chart placement. The catch: layouts are functional, not magazine-quality — for a high-stakes external pitch, treat this as the starting point a designer polishes.
Three more surfaces

Beyond the deck.

🎥 Gemini in Meet

Turn on "Take notes for me" in the call controls; at the end, Gemini emails the recap — summary, decisions, action items — to attendees.

Accurate, not perfect — spot-check critical decisions against the recording for high-stakes meetings.

📱 Mobile-native captures

Voice-input notes on the walk back, photograph a receipt or whiteboard to transcribe, quick research between calls, live translation.

Don't do deep work on mobile — it's for quick captures and answers. Move multi-step work to desktop.

📅 Calendar & Tasks

"Book a 30-min sync next Tuesday afternoon," "re-prioritize my Tasks from these emails," "block 4 hours of focus time."

It uses your access — it can't see others' calendars unless they've shared them.
Your call · which surface?

Match the job to the surface.

Use all four surfaces in one week

For one work week, deliberately use Gemini in Slides, Meet, mobile, and Calendar at least once each. Track which actually save time and which are just novelty — keep the wins, drop the rest.
