Workspace agents: what's useful, what's noise.

Gemini's integration across Google Workspace is broad but uneven. Some apps got real agentic capability; others got "help me write" buttons that don't change what's possible. This is the honest map — and a sorting test, because the only skill that matters here is telling a real time-saver from decoration.

The one rule

Transform, don't generate.

Across every Workspace app, the same line divides useful from noise. Gemini is excellent at transforming things you already have — summarize, restructure, find inconsistencies, clean, translate. It's weak at generating from blank — first drafts, "make this more professional," auto-replies. The second category produces generic, AI-clichéd output that's rarely better than your own.

Quick reference

The three apps, condensed.

📄 Docs — the most mature
TrustSummarize 2,000+ word docs · find inconsistencies · build a TOC from headings · convert to a slide outline · translate keeping formatting.
Verify"Help me write" from blank · "improve this paragraph" · "make this more professional" (adds clichés).
📊 Sheets — more than you think
TrustBuild formulas (INDEX/MATCH, XLOOKUP) · clean messy data · natural-language pivot tables · suggest a chart type.
VerifyVerify formulas on a small range first · "find patterns" = starting point not conclusion · no real statistical inference.
✉️ Gmail — triage, not writing
TrustSummarize long threads · "what is this asking me to do?" · semantic search · schedule from thread context.
VerifyAuto-drafts over-apologize and over-pad — starting point only, never final · auto-archive has too many false positives.
The Sheets trap

A Gemini-suggested formula can use wrong cell references if your data isn't shaped the way it inferred. Always test it on a small range before applying it across thousands of rows.

New surface · 26 August 2026

The fourth app: Ask Gemini in Google Chat.

From 26 August 2026, Google Chat gets Ask Gemini — a single command line inside Chat, powered by Workspace Intelligence, that searches across your Workspace data (Gmail, Drive, Calendar), drafts content and images without leaving the conversation, catches you up on discussions, schedules meetings and manages tasks, and keeps separate sessions so you can return to a topic later. You will find it under Shortcuts on the left, or with Ctrl + G (⌘ + G on macOS).

This one takes something away

Ask Gemini replaces the Gemini side panel in Chat — after the launch the side panel is gone from Chat, and Gems are no longer reachable from it (Gems stay available in the Gemini side panel of the other Workspace apps). Your existing side-panel conversation history will not migrate; admins can export it, and users can download it where permitted, but note it lives under “Gemini in Workspace” in Takeout, not under Google Chat. If you have a Chat-side-panel habit worth keeping, export before the rollout reaches you.

Two eligibility details that decide whether you see it at all. It only affects accounts whose Google account language is English — other languages keep the side panel for now. And availability is Business Standard and Plus, Enterprise Standard and Plus, and Google AI Pro for Education, with the admin needing Gemini in Workspace in Chat and Workspace Intelligence enabled, and the user enrolled in Workspace Smart Features. Rollout is gradual, up to 15 days for the feature to appear, starting 26 August.

Through 1 October 2026 Google is granting promotional access to higher Ask Gemini limits so people can experiment; usage limits apply after that date. Plan pilots accordingly rather than assuming the free-for-all is permanent.
