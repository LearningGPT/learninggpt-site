AI Coach
Claude wins this round. It referenced a specific pain point from the demo — "reporting eats 4 hours of your Friday" — instead of a generic check-in. That specificity signals the sender was actually listening, and the "totally fine either way" removes pressure without sounding desperate. ChatGPT was close but stayed vague. Gemini defaulted to corporate pleasantries. The lesson: the best follow-up emails reference a specific friction the prospect named — not the product you're selling.
