Meta AI in WhatsApp: tame the group chats.

WhatsApp is where most people meet Meta AI. Beyond a chatbot, it can summarize the threads you have been ignoring and answer right inside a conversation. Here is how to use it without the noise.

01The basics

  1. Open the Meta AI chat (or tap the AI icon) to ask anything privately, like any assistant.
  2. Inside a normal chat, invoke Meta AI to draft a reply or answer a question without leaving the conversation.
  3. For a busy group, ask for a summary — "catch me up on this thread" — to get the gist in seconds.
The morning catch-up

The killer use: open a group that blew up overnight and ask Meta AI to summarize what you missed and surface anything that needs your reply. You go from 200 unread to a 30-second brief.

02Draft in your voice

Ask it to draft a reply and tell it the tone — "warm but brief," "say no politely." You review and send, so you stay in control of what actually goes out under your name.

Summaries and AI features touch your messages. Remember it is operating inside WhatsApp — keep genuinely sensitive conversations out of AI features, and check your privacy settings.
Frequently asked

Meta AI — your questions, answered

How do I use Meta AI in WhatsApp?
Open the Meta AI chat to ask anything, or invoke it inside a normal chat to draft replies and answer questions without leaving the conversation.
Can Meta AI summarize WhatsApp group chats?
Yes — ask it to catch you up on a thread and it returns the gist plus anything that needs your reply, which is ideal for busy groups.
Can Meta AI write my WhatsApp replies?
Yes — ask it to draft a reply in a tone you specify; you review and send it yourself.
Is Meta AI in WhatsApp private?
It operates inside WhatsApp and its AI features touch your messages, so keep sensitive conversations out of AI features and review your privacy settings.
