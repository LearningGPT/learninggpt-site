Questions we hear a lot

Why not just have our people use the free vendor training?

Because vendor training only teaches their tool. If your engineers use Claude, your marketers use ChatGPT, and your finance team uses Copilot, you'd need three different training programs — each one telling your team their tool is best. LearningGPT teaches all of them honestly, in one place, with the same standards.

Can you build custom content for our company?

For pilot customers, yes — we do a curriculum review where we look at your specific tools, workflows, and use cases, and recommend the lesson sequence (or build a custom one) that fits your team. Larger enterprise customers can request fully bespoke lessons; we'll quote that separately.

How do you keep the content current as models change?

Lessons are versioned. When a model update changes a workflow, we update the lesson and (starting Q3 2026) push a skill-update alert to every learner who's completed that lesson. The curriculum is continuously maintained, not a one-shot course.

Do you offer invoicing and PO terms?

For Team and Department tiers, we accept credit card via Stripe by default. For Enterprise (100+ seats), we accept ACH and POs with Net-30 terms. Email us with your procurement requirements.

What about SSO, SCIM, and admin dashboards?

On the roadmap for late 2026. Pilot customers help shape what we build. If specific compliance or admin features are deal-breakers for you today, tell us — we triage based on what real customers need.
