Slides, Meet, advanced: the rest of Workspace.

The lesson covering everything else Gemini does in Workspace beyond Gmail/Docs/Sheets. Gemini in Slides for presentations, Meet for live note-taking and transcription, mobile workflows, and integration with Calendar and Tasks. By the end, you'll have covered every meaningful Gemini surface in Workspace.

The mental model

Gemini is everywhere in Workspace; learn to use the surfaces beyond Gmail and Docs.

Most Gemini users stick to the core surfaces (Gmail, Docs, Sheets). But Gemini in Slides, Meet, Calendar, and on mobile each has its own win. This lesson covers the four that compound on top of what you already know.

Workflow 01 Gemini in Slides: from outline to deck

1

Generate slide content from natural language

Most people fight Slides templates for hours. With Gemini, you describe what you want and get a draft deck to refine.

The prompt that works

Slide-generation promptGenerate a 10-slide deck on [topic] for [audience]. Include: title, agenda, 3 key points each with a slide, a data slide (suggest the chart), Q&A, contact slide. Match a clean, professional style.

Best use cases

  • Sales decks and proposals
  • Internal presentations
  • Training materials
  • Speaker decks for events
Generated slide layouts are functional but not magazine-quality. For high-stakes external presentations, treat Gemini's output as a starting point that a designer will polish.
Time savings: Deck-from-scratch: 4 hours → 30 min including polish.

Workflow 02 Gemini in Meet: live transcription + summary

2

Auto-transcribe and summarize meetings

Enable Gemini in Meet. Recording with auto-transcription captures everything. After the meeting, Gemini generates the summary, action items, and decisions.

The prompt that works

SetupIn any Meet call → click the Gemini icon in the call controls → enable "Take notes for me" → at end of meeting, Gemini delivers the recap to all attendees via email.

Best use cases

  • All-hands and team meetings where notes matter
  • Customer calls where action items need follow-up
  • Interviews and 1:1s for personal records
  • Multi-stakeholder meetings where alignment matters
Meeting notes from Gemini are accurate but not perfect. Spot-check critical decisions and action items against the recording for high-stakes meetings.
Time savings: Meeting note-taking: gone, structured.

Workflow 03 Mobile workflows: Gemini on the phone

3

The use cases that make sense on mobile

Gemini on iOS/Android isn't just smaller-screen ChatGPT. There are specific workflows that work great on mobile and bad on desktop.

The prompt that works

Mobile-native workflowsVoice-input notes after meetings on the walk back. Photograph receipts/whiteboards for instant transcription. Quick research between calls without opening a laptop. Live translation in conversations or while reading.

Best use cases

  • Field sales reps
  • Anyone who's frequently away from their desk
  • Travelers needing translation help
  • Note-taking after offsite meetings
Don't try to do deep work on mobile. Mobile Gemini is for quick captures and quick answers. Move to desktop for anything multi-step.
Time savings: Dead time on phone: productive thinking time.

Workflow 04 Calendar and Tasks integration

4

Let Gemini schedule and plan

Gemini integrates with Calendar and Tasks to help with scheduling, planning, and follow-through.

The prompt that works

Integration promptsSchedule a 30-min sync with [person] for next week, ideally Tuesday afternoon. Add it to my Calendar. Review my Tasks for this week. Re-prioritize based on these emails I just received. What does my Calendar look like for the next two weeks? Block 4 hours of focus time.

Best use cases

  • Manager scheduling many meetings
  • Planning weeks with many constraints
  • Maintaining personal task lists
  • Pre-meeting prep automation
Calendar integration uses your access permissions. It can't see others' calendars unless they've shared. Gemini works with what it can see.
Time savings: Calendar admin and scheduling: dramatically reduced.

Final challenge: use all 4 surfaces in one week

For one work week, deliberately use Gemini in Slides, Meet, mobile, and Calendar at least once each. Track which surfaces actually save time vs. which are novelty. Keep the wins, drop the rest.
