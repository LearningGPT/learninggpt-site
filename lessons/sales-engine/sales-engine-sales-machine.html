Your sales machine — the pipeline runs, you stay human.

A sales machine isn’t about removing yourself — it’s about removing the busywork around you. This lesson connects research, outreach, and your CRM into one system: leads arrive enriched, sequences trigger and pause themselves, follow-ups never slip — with you approving cold sends and handling every real conversation.

The mental model

Connect research, outreach, and your CRM into one system — so the pipeline runs while you do the human conversations.

A sales machine isn’t about removing yourself; it’s about removing the busywork around you. AI enriches records, drafts sequences, and tracks follow-ups; you make the calls, build the relationships, and approve what goes out. The system handles volume; you handle judgment.

Step 01 Enrich your CRM automatically

Point your research step at your CRM so new leads arrive already researched — company, role, trigger, and a suggested angle filled in. No more cold records.

Step 02 Sequences that trigger themselves

Wire research → personalized draft → sequence, kicked off by an event (a new lead, a form fill, a download). The system prepares everything.

Sequence-automation promptWhen a new lead is added to [CRM], research them, draft a personalized first touch and a 3-step sequence using my templates, and queue them as drafts marked “needs approval.” Don’t send anything automatically.

Step 03 Follow-up and reply routing

Automate the discipline: reminders when it’s time to follow up, sequences that pause the moment someone replies, and replies routed to you fast. The machine never forgets to follow up; you never miss a live conversation.

Step 04 Measure, and stay human and compliant

  • Measure — reply rates by message and segment; double down on what works.
  • Stay human — a person approves cold sends and handles every real reply.
  • Stay compliant — opt-outs honored automatically, sending limits respected, laws followed.
Fully automated cold outreach — AI researching, writing, and sending at volume with no human gate — is the fast track to a wrecked sender reputation, blocked domains, and legal trouble. Keep a human approving cold sends, respect every opt-out and the law, and treat deliverability as sacred. See the Secure Your AI Use build for handling prospect data responsibly.

Your challenge: wire the pipeline

Connect the pieces from Lessons 1 and 2:

  1. Auto-enrich new CRM leads with research.
  2. Trigger a personalized sequence as drafts marked “needs approval.”
  3. Automate follow-up reminders and pause-on-reply.
  4. Set up reply rate tracking; confirm opt-outs and limits are enforced.

That’s a sales engine that handles volume while you handle relationships — and stays compliant. You’ve finished the Build a Sales Engine track.
