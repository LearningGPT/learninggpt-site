Deep Research: 20-source briefings.

Perplexity's Deep Research mode does 10-15 minutes of multi-source investigation before answering. It's the closest thing to having a research analyst on staff. This lesson covers when Deep Research wins over regular search, how to prompt it for real depth, and how to evaluate the output.

The mental model

Deep Research is for the questions you'd otherwise hire an analyst for.

Not for facts (regular search is faster). Not for creativity (use chat AIs). For: market analyses, competitive landscapes, due-diligence-type questions where 10-30 sources need to be synthesized with citations.

Workflow 01 Prompt Deep Research for real reports

1

Specific structure, broad coverage

Vague prompts produce vague reports. Give Deep Research a clear structure.

The prompt that works

Good Deep Research promptsResearch the enterprise AI training market in 2026. Cover: market size estimates with sources, top 5 players and their positioning, pricing models, recent funding events, predicted growth and key drivers, regulatory considerations.

Best use cases

  • Market analyses and competitive landscapes
  • Due diligence on companies or topics
  • Regulatory or policy research
  • Pre-decision deep dives
Deep Research takes 10-15 minutes. Don't expect instant results.
Time savings: Hours of analyst time: 15 min of Perplexity time.

Workflow 02 Use follow-up to drill in

2

First report is the starting point, not the destination

Once Deep Research finishes, ask follow-up questions to dig into specific sections.

The prompt that works

Follow-up pattern(After report) Tell me more about [specific player]. What's their pricing? Who funds them? Recent customer wins? (Drilling further) Find an interview where their CEO discussed strategy in the last 6 months.

Best use cases

  • Iterative deep dives
  • Targeted follow-ups on specific findings
  • Building a research narrative incrementally
Each follow-up may trigger another Deep Research run. Budget accordingly if you're on metered plans.
Time savings: Layered research: hours → tens of minutes.

Workflow 03 Evaluate before trusting

3

The three-source check

Don't trust Deep Research output without verification on the points that matter.

The prompt that works

Three-source check(1) Pick 3 important claims. Click their citations. Read the actual source. (2) Check recency — is anything time-sensitive based on stale data? (3) Check absences — did it miss something obvious you know about?

Best use cases

  • Anything you'll publish or present externally
  • Decisions that depend on the research being right
  • Building trust calibration over time
Deep Research sometimes cites SEO-spam alongside high-quality sources. The check protects you from leaning on bad inputs.
Time savings: Catastrophic errors from bad sources: prevented.

Final challenge: one real Deep Research project

Pick a real question you've been meaning to research. Run Deep Research. Do at least 2 follow-ups. Verify 3 claims against sources. Compare to what you'd have produced manually in 2-3 hours.
