What it costs: the honest c.ai+ math.

Character.AI’s free tier got noticeably less free in 2026 — ads mid-chat, and a currency called Charms metering features that used to be unlimited. Here’s exactly what changed, what $9.99 buys, and a clear rule for who should pay.

01The free tier in 2026

Free still gets you the core: unlimited basic chatting with any public character, character creation, personas. What changed: ads appear mid-conversation, swiping for alternate responses now spends Charms (a metered virtual currency), and some formerly-free features moved behind the subscription. Perfectly fine for casual use; friction grows with daily use.

02What c.ai+ buys

$9.99/month, or $94.99/year (about $7.92/month — roughly 20% off). You get: no ads, priority access with faster responses, the newest models, unlimited voice calls, longer character definitions, better character memory, early feature access, and no Charm-counting.

The decision rule

Chat a few times a week? Stay free and ignore the ads. Daily user, voice caller, or serious character creator? The annual plan at ~$7.92/month is the honest buy — the monthly-to-annual gap is one skipped coffee.

03Cost per hour of use

Because sessions on Character.AI run long, the per-hour math is almost comically cheap: a daily user paying annual pricing lands under 30 cents per hour of use — less than any streaming service. The real cost to watch is not money but time: this platform is engineered to be engaging, and the safety lesson covers keeping that healthy.

Frequently asked

Pricing — FAQs

How much is c.ai+?
$9.99/month or $94.99/year (≈$7.92/month, about 20% savings on annual).
What are Charms?
The 2026 free tier’s metered currency — spent on features like response swipes. Subscribers don’t count Charms.
Is the free tier still usable?
Yes — core chatting is unlimited, with mid-chat ads and Charm limits. Casual users lose little; daily users feel the friction.
Is there a Character.AI API?
The product is consumer-first; there is no official general API comparable to OpenAI or Anthropic. Businesses wanting character-style AI typically build on standard LLM APIs.
