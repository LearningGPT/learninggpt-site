A bot that actually helps.

An FAQ bot answers the easy ones. A helpful bot feeds on your real docs, hands off to a human gracefully when it can't help, and captures leads — without trapping anyone. This lesson is the difference between a deflection wall and real support.

The mental model

Helpful means three things: real knowledge, a graceful exit to a human, and a way to follow up.

A bot earns trust by knowing your actual documentation, by never trapping someone who needs a person, and by capturing the conversation so a human can follow up. Knowledge, handoff, and capture — that's the whole upgrade from FAQ toy to real support.

The reframeThe escape hatch is a feature, not a failure. A bot that can't reach a human when needed is a trap. Make the handoff easy and obvious — it's what makes people trust the bot enough to use it.
