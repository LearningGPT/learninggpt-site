Perplexity for sales: 5-minute prospect prep.

B2B sales reps are Perplexity's best-fit users, and most of them haven't realized it yet. The four-prompt routine below turns 45 minutes of LinkedIn-spelunking into a 5-minute briefing that's sharper than what most sales orgs train their reps to do. Build it once, run it before every call.

The mental model

Sales research is repetitive — that's the edge.

Every prospect needs the same handful of angles: company context, recent news, the decision-maker's background, the competitive landscape. Because it's the same every time, you save the prompts once and reuse them on every account forever. Let's build the briefing live.

Verify before you trust it

Perplexity pulls from public sources, so time-sensitive and private-company data drifts — revenue estimates, headcount, and profile details especially. Cross-check the flagged items against LinkedIn or a filing before you quote a number or a name on a live call. Competitive intel from public sources is a starting point, not the answer.
