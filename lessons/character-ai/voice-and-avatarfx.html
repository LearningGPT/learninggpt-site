Voice & AvatarFX: characters you can hear and see.

Two features moved Character.AI beyond text: voice calls that feel like phoning your character, and AvatarFX — give it one image and it produces video of that face talking, emoting, even singing. Here’s how to use both well.

01Voice: call your character

Any character can talk. Pick from a library of voices (or characters ship with one chosen by their creator), hit the call button, and speak naturally — it listens and answers in real time. Language practice is the killer use: speaking Spanish to a patient tutor beats typing it by a mile. Free tier meters calls; c.ai+ makes them unlimited.

Best first call

A language tutor or interview coach — voice turns rehearsal into the real thing. Ten minutes out loud is worth an hour of typed practice.

02AvatarFX: one image, living video

AvatarFX takes a single image — portrait, painting, pet, cartoon — plus audio or a script, and generates video of it speaking with matched lip-sync, expressions, and gestures. It handles multiple speakers and holds consistency across longer clips. The uncanny power comes with a rule: only animate images you have the right to use — your own creations, not photos of real people without their consent.

Animating a real person’s photo without permission is where fun becomes deepfake. The platform has guardrails, but your judgment is the real one.

03Scenes, Streams, and the feed

Scenes drop your character into a pre-built cinematic moment; Streams let you pick any two characters and a topic, hit play, and watch them interact. Creations can be shared to the community feed — which is also the best place to see what skilled creators are doing before you build your own.

Frequently asked

Voice & video — FAQs

Can I talk to characters out loud?
Yes — voice calls work with any character in real time. Free tier meters them; c.ai+ calls are unlimited.
What is AvatarFX?
Character.AI’s video generator: one image plus audio becomes video of that face speaking with lip-sync, expression, and gesture — even multi-speaker conversations.
What are Streams?
Pick two characters and a topic, press play, and watch them interact — shareable to the community feed.
Can I animate a photo of a real person?
Only with their consent. Platform guardrails exist, but the ethical line is yours to hold — stick to images you have rights to.
