Earning with AI Pro ~13 min · hands-on Recurring service

One video, a week of content.

By Dan Allen — ex-Microsoft, Microsoft Certified: AI Business Professional

Every creator, coach, and founder has the same problem: they make one good long video or podcast and then post nothing for a week because cutting it up is miserable. You solve that — one long video becomes clips, posts, a newsletter, show notes, and a thread — and it's the kind of repeatable, painful job people happily pay a monthly retainer for. AI does the heavy lifting; you run the pipeline.

01 What you're really selling: consistency

You're not selling "video editing." You're selling showing up everywhere, every week, without the client lifting a finger. They record once; you turn that single asset into a week of presence across platforms. That's a recurring need — which is why this is a retainer, not a one-off, and why it stacks into real monthly income.

For the clips themselves, a tool like Opus Clip or Descript auto-finds the best moments and adds captions; you review and tidy. The whole week's batch is a couple of focused hours — for a deliverable a client values at hundreds.

Where your value sits

AI drafts the posts and finds rough clip moments; your taste picks the genuinely good ones and nails the client's voice. Anyone can dump 20 mediocre AI clips. What earns the retainer is curation — shipping the 5 that actually land, consistently, so the client trusts you with their face and never has to look.

05 Where the clients are

Your clients are people who already publish but inconsistently. Find them by looking for the gap: a podcaster who hasn't clipped an episode in a month, a coach posting once then going dark, a founder with a great talk and no follow-up. DM them something specific — "I turned your last episode into 5 clips, here are two; want the rest every week?" Doing the work first, unasked, closes these.

Your move this week

Pick one creator you admire who posts inconsistently. Take their latest video, actually run it through the pipeline, and make three real clips and two posts. Send two, free, with "there's a week of this in every episode — want me to run it?" One yes is a retainer.
