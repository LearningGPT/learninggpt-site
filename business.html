Questions
Common questions about business plans.
What's the minimum commitment?
Business Pro and Pro+ require a minimum of 3 seats. The Power Add-on requires a minimum of 25 seats and must be added to an existing business plan. No maximum — contact us for volume pricing above 200 seats.
Do you offer annual billing?
Yes — annual contracts save significantly vs monthly. Business Pro annual is ~$58/user/year (save 19%). Business Pro+ annual is $99/user/year (save 41%) — under $100 per employee for a full year of AI training. Contact us to set up annual billing.
What are the role-based emails?
Every week, each employee receives a short email with one AI prompt, one tip, and one LearningGPT lesson relevant to their job role — Sales, HR, Engineering, Marketing, Finance, or Operations. Content is written for practitioners, not tech people.
What are the quarterly office hours?
Pro+ business accounts get a dedicated 1-hour session with Dan each quarter — just for your team. Bring questions about AI tools, workflows, prompting strategy, or anything from the curriculum. Sessions are recorded and shared afterward.
Can employees keep their individual accounts?
Yes — if an employee already has an individual Pro or Pro+ subscription, their account upgrades to the business plan. Their progress carries over.
How does billing work for teams?
One invoice per company, billed monthly or annually. Add or remove seats at any time — additions are prorated to your billing cycle. We handle the math.
Do you offer a trial?
Yes — reach out and we'll set up a 2-week trial for your team on the Business Pro plan. No credit card required to start the trial.
Is there an enterprise option?
For teams over 200, we offer custom pricing, dedicated onboarding, a personal kickoff session with our founder, and a custom billing arrangement. Contact our team to discuss what works for your organization.
