Questions

Do I need a credit card to start free?
No. The free Explorer plan requires no credit card — ever.
What's the difference between monthly and annual?
Annual plans save you 26% on Pro ($79/yr vs $108/yr) and 34% on Pro+ ($149/yr vs $228/yr). Annual members also get priority access to new features.
What's a founding member?
Anyone who subscribes before September 30th, 2026 is a founding member. You lock in today's pricing forever — when we raise prices after launch, your rate never changes.
What are the monthly office hours?
A live 1-hour session with Dan, open to all Pro+ members. Come with questions about AI tools, workflows, prompting, or anything covered in the curriculum. Sessions are recorded and posted for anyone who can't attend live. Starting Q3 2026.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Monthly plans cancel anytime. Annual plans can be cancelled to stop renewal — you keep access through the end of your paid year.
What payment methods do you accept?
All major credit and debit cards via Stripe. Your card details are never stored on our servers.
Can I switch between monthly and annual?
Yes. Switching from monthly to annual takes effect immediately — Stripe credits any unused days from your current month toward the annual price, so you only pay the difference. Switching from annual to monthly works differently: you keep full access through the end of your paid year, and monthly billing starts at your next renewal date. Annual plans are not refunded mid-year.
