What you can do with AI

Helpful, everyday things — with the exact words to type. Copy one, change the details, and try it.

✉️

Write letters & emails

A thank-you note, a message to your doctor, a complaint to a company — written clearly, the way you mean it.

Try typingHelp me write a friendly thank-you note to my granddaughter for the birthday gift she sent. Keep it warm and a few sentences long.
❓

Ask anything, in plain words

Get clear answers without the confusing parts. Always double-check anything important with your doctor or a person you trust.

Try typingExplain in simple terms what my doctor means by [the term]. Keep it short and easy to understand. (Then ask your doctor to be sure.)
📱

Get help with everyday tech

Stuck on your phone, email, or the TV remote? Describe the problem and get patient, step-by-step help.

Try typingExplain it to me like I'm not a computer person, one step at a time: how do I [the thing you're stuck on] on my [phone/iPad/computer]?
👨‍👩‍👧

Stay close to family

Write a message to the grandkids, plan a visit, or come up with thoughtful questions to ask on your next call.

Try typingGive me a few warm, fun questions to ask my teenage grandson about his life so we have a good chat on the phone.
🌷

Enjoy your hobbies

Recipes, gardening tips, history, crossword help, trip ideas — a patient helper for whatever you're curious about.

Try typingI have chicken, rice, and a few vegetables. Give me one simple dinner recipe with easy steps and short instructions.
🛡️

Check if something's a scam

Got a suspicious message or call? Let AI take a look before you do anything — a free second opinion.

Try typingI got this message: [paste it]. Does this look like a scam? What should I watch out for, and what should I do?
