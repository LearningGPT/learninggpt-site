ChatGPT for email: write 10 emails in the time of 1.

ChatGPT isn't wired into your inbox the way Copilot is — but it has a different superpower: memory and customization. With one Custom GPT and a few habits, it becomes a personal email copywriter that knows your voice, your situations, and your style. Five workflows, one 10-minute setup that compounds across every email you'll ever write.

The mental model

ChatGPT remembers things. Use that.

The biggest mistake people make: starting every email session with a blank chat and a one-line prompt. With no context about you, your audience, or your style, ChatGPT produces the most-average-possible output — and you burn three minutes editing it back toward something you'd actually send.

Three features let you bake context in once and reuse it forever: Custom Instructions (global preferences), Memory (what it learns about you over time), and Custom GPTs (purpose-built mini-assistants). But first, the habit that matters most in a single prompt.

Two prompts for the same follow-up email. Which one returns a draft you can send with almost no editing?
Right. The second prompt names the person, the friction, the goal, and the constraints — so ChatGPT has something real to work with instead of guessing. One specific detail from the prior interaction is worth more than any amount of "make it good." That's the whole game, and you'll see the flip side of it next: scrubbing the generic tone out.

The 10-minute setup

A Custom GPT that already knows you.

The investment that pays off forever: a Custom GPT called "My Email Assistant" that knows your job, your common email types, and your voice. Open it instead of vanilla ChatGPT for anything email-related.

  1. chatgpt.com → Explore GPTs → Create
  2. Name it "My Email Assistant"
  3. Paste this into Instructions and customize it:
You are my personal email copywriter. About me: I'm [your role] at [company]. I mostly email [audience]. My voice: - Conversational, not stiff · direct, no "circling back" - Use contractions · avoid buzzwords (synergy, leverage, touch base) Default rules: - Replies under 100 words unless I say otherwise - Always end with a clear next step or ask - If I paste an email I received, match its tone - Never leave [Name]/[Company] placeholders — blank line if info is missing

Add 2-3 conversation starters, save it Private, and it lives in your sidebar permanently — dramatically better than vanilla ChatGPT because the context is already there.

Keep Custom GPT instructions about style and process, not specific deals. Don't bake in customer names or financial details — the instructions are stored.
And Memory

When you catch yourself explaining the same thing twice — your industry, your subject-line rules, what "draft a follow-up" means to you — tell ChatGPT to remember it. Six months later it's still applied automatically, across every chat. (Settings → Personalization. Glance at it occasionally and prune anything it stored that you didn't mean.)
