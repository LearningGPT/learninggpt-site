Grok's maker is now SpaceXAI.

If you visited x.ai this month and did a double-take at the header — "SpaceXAI, creators of Grok" — you're caught up on the branding but not the story. The company behind Grok was acquired by SpaceX back in February; July 2026 is when the public-facing rebrand landed. Here's the timeline, what actually changes for you (very little), and the ship list from a very busy summer.

01 The timeline, straight

DateWhat happened
Feb 2, 2026SpaceX announces it has acquired xAI ("xAI joins SpaceX")
Spring 2026Business as usual under the xAI name — Grok Build, Skills, Connectors, voice APIs all ship
July 2026The public rebrand: the website, news, and social presence switch to SpaceXAI
Naming nuance: the legal entity is still "xAI Corp" (check any page footer), the API console still lives at console.x.ai, and the docs still say x.ai. "SpaceXAI" is the consumer-facing brand. Expect a messy year of both names appearing — including in our own lessons where the docs still say xAI.

02 What changes for you

Today: nothing breaks. grok.com works, the iOS/Android apps update normally, Grok on X is unchanged, your SuperGrok or Premium+ subscription carries over, and the API endpoints are untouched. This is a brand change, not a platform migration — no action required.

The longer game is worth understanding, though. SpaceX owning a frontier AI lab means Grok's compute story (the Colossus supercomputers) and its funding now sit inside one of the world's most aggressive engineering companies. That's the bull case. The judgment layer this track always teaches still applies: the data dials and honest assessment don't change because the logo did.

04 The honest read

Two things are true at once. Grok is shipping faster than any other frontier lab right now — voices, agents, integrations, add-ins, week after week. And it still has the loosest product culture in frontier AI, which is exactly why this track pairs every capability lesson with a judgment layer. A rocket company's ambition doesn't change either fact. Use the firehose; keep the evidence discipline.

Five-minute check

Open your Grok data-sharing settings today (grok.com → Settings → Data). Ownership changes are precisely when quiet policy updates happen — this one took about five months to reach the logo. Confirm your training-data dials are where you set them, and skim the privacy policy's "last updated" date while you're there.
