Build a repeatable sales machine.

Doing it once is a skill; doing it every week without reinventing it is a system. This lesson turns research, outreach, and follow-up into a repeatable machine that fills your pipeline — personalized at scale, without sounding generic.

The mental model

A sales machine is a fixed sequence with a personalized core — the steps repeat, the specifics don't.

The system is the same every time: research → first touch → follow-up → follow-up → break. What changes per prospect is the one researched detail at the center of each message. Standardize the sequence; personalize the hook. That's how you scale without becoming spam.

The reframeScale the steps, not the sameness. Automation should repeat the process, never the message. The moment two prospects get the identical email, you're spam.

Step 02 · Personalize at scale — without the generic trap

The whole game is staying specific as volume rises. Tap each setup — does it scale and stay personal, or tip into spam?

The machine fails the moment it goes fully generic or fully automatic. Keep a human eye on the drafts and respect opt-outs and sending limits — blasting unreviewed, identical mail at scale is how you land in spam folders and burn your domain. Personalize at scale means personalize, at scale.

Your challenge: stand up the machine

  1. Define your sequence: research → touch 1 → touch 2 → touch 3.
  2. Build a template for each touch with a slot for the researched hook.
  3. Batch-research five real prospects and personalize each opener.
  4. Track who's at which step and review every draft before it sends.
