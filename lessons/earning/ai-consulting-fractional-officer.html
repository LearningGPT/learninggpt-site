Earning with AI Pro+ ~15 min · hands-on Premium advisory

Sell the decision, not the work.

Every business owner knows AI matters and has no idea what to actually do about it. They're drowning in tools, hype, and fear of falling behind — and they'll pay a premium for someone who can cut through it and tell them exactly where to apply AI, what to ignore, and how. That's consulting, and it's the highest margin play in this track: no production treadmill, just your judgment, sold as strategy sessions, roadmaps, training, and a fractional "AI officer" retainer.

01 You're selling clarity, not labor

The automation agency builds things; the consultant decides which things to build and whether they're worth it. You sell the scarce thing: judgment. Which tools fit this business, where AI will actually save money versus where it's a distraction, the roadmap to roll it out, how to train the team, and the expensive mistakes to skip. Because it's advice and not delivery, your overhead is near zero and your rate reflects the value of being right, not hours worked.

02 The offer ladder

Productize your advice into clear, escalating offers so a prospect can start small and grow:

Fractional AI officerOngoing advisor on retainer — the recurring crown jewel$2,000–$8,000/mo
The fractional AI officer is the prize

Most SMBs can't justify a full-time head of AI, but they badly need one. You become that person for several companies at once — a few hours a month each, setting direction, vetting tools, keeping them current, and steering the team. Recurring, high-margin, and sticky, because the field changes every month and they rely on you to keep up so they don't have to. Land via an audit, prove value, convert to the retainer.

03 You don't need to be the world's top expert

The blocker people invent is "who am I to consult?" Here's the truth: you only need to be meaningfully ahead of your client and able to translate AI into their business. A business owner running a plumbing company or a law firm is years behind on AI and has no time to catch up. Being a credible, current guide who speaks their language and gives them a clear plan is the entire job — and it's worth a lot to someone with no idea where to start. Confidence and communication matter as much as raw expertise.

04 Pick a lane — it's what makes you premium

"AI consultant" is a commodity; "AI for law firms" or "AI for e-commerce ops" is a specialist who commands real rates and referrals. Pick an industry or a function you understand (ideally from past work), and become the person known for AI there. Specialists out-earn generalists in consulting more than anywhere — depth is the whole pitch.

05 Land your first client

The fastest path: lead with a free or low-cost mini-audit for a business in your lane. Spend an hour mapping where they're losing time or could apply AI, deliver a one-page prioritized plan, and the paid engagement (or the retainer) is the obvious next step. You've already demonstrated the exact value you sell. Find them through your network, LinkedIn (post insights for your niche), and local business groups.

Your move this week

Pick one industry you understand. Write a one-page "AI opportunity audit" template for it — the questions you'd ask, the common high-ROI plays, the mistakes to avoid. Then offer a free audit to one real business in that lane and deliver it. That document is your product, your credibility, and your sales pitch in one.
