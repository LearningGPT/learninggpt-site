📣 Marketing & getting customers

1. A month of social posts

Give me 12 social media post ideas for my [type] business, with a short caption for each. Mix promotions, tips, behind-the-scenes, and customer wins. Friendly, local, not salesy.

2. An ad that gets clicks

Write 3 versions of a Facebook/Google ad for [product/service], for [target customer] in [area]. Lead with the benefit, end with a clear call to action.

3. Win back past customers

Write a short, warm email to past customers I haven't seen in a while, offering [reason to return / offer]. No pressure, just a friendly nudge.

4. Homepage copy that converts

Write clear homepage copy for my [type] business: a headline, a sentence on what I do and for whom, 3 reasons to choose me, and a call to action.

5. Reply to any review

Write a professional reply to this [5-star / negative] review: [paste]. Grateful and specific for good ones; calm, accountable, and solution-focused for bad ones.

💬 Customer service

6. Build your FAQ

Here are the questions customers ask most: [list]. Write clear, friendly answers I can put on my website or hand to staff.

7. Handle a complaint well

A customer is upset about [issue]. Write a reply that hears them out, takes responsibility where fair, and offers a specific fix. Keep the relationship.

8. Canned replies that don't sound canned

Write 6 quick, friendly reply templates for common customer messages (hours, pricing, availability, thank-you, follow-up, refund) that still sound human.

💵 Sales, quotes & getting paid

9. Follow up a lead

Write a short follow-up to a customer who asked about [service] but hasn't booked. Helpful, no pressure, easy for them to say yes.

10. A proposal in minutes

Turn these notes into a simple one-page proposal for [customer]: what I'll do, the price, the timeline, and why me. Confident and clear.

11. Chase an invoice

Write a polite but firm reminder for an invoice that's [X] days overdue. Keep it professional and make the next step obvious.

🗂️ Admin & operations

12. Write the SOP

Turn how I do [task] into a clear step-by-step SOP a new employee could follow without asking me. [Describe the process.]

13. A job ad that attracts good people

Write a job posting for a [role] at my [type] business: what the work is, what I'm looking for, the vibe of the place, and how to apply.

14. New-hire onboarding checklist

Create a first-week onboarding checklist for a new [role], covering paperwork, tools, training, and who they should meet.

15. Policy in plain English

Write a short, friendly [refund / cancellation / late] policy for my business that's fair, clear, and won't scare customers off.

📊 Finance & decisions

16. Explain the numbers

Here's my [sales / expense] data: [paste]. Tell me the 3 things I should pay attention to, in plain language. What's trending up or down?

17. Think through a decision

I'm deciding whether to [hire / raise prices / add a service]. Ask me the 4 questions that would actually decide it, then help me weigh it.

18. Compare two options

Compare [option A] vs [option B] for my business — I care most about [cost / time / growth]. Give me a straight recommendation.

✍️ Content & everyday

19. A blog post from one idea

Write a helpful, plain-English blog post for my customers about [topic]. Practical, not preachy, with a soft mention of what I offer at the end.

20. The weekly newsletter

Draft a short, friendly email newsletter for my customers: one useful tip, one update, and one reason to come back.

21. Product/service descriptions

Write clear, appealing descriptions for these [products/services]: [list]. Benefit-focused, honest, easy to skim.

22. Summarize the long thing

Summarize this [contract / report / email thread]: the key points, anything I need to decide, and anything that looks risky. Plain language.

23. Meeting notes into actions

Turn these messy meeting notes into a clean list of decisions and next steps with owners: [paste].

24. The tricky message

Help me write a message to [employee / vendor / customer] about [sensitive issue]. Honest, respectful, and focused on a solution.

25. The "make this better" catch-all

Here's something I wrote for my business. Make it clearer and more professional, keep it in my voice, and tell me the one change that matters most.
