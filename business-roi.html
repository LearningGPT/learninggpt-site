How this is calculated (and why it's conservative)

AI spend / year = team size × tool cost × 12. Spend not returning value assumes a tool only pays off for people who use it well — so the rest of your license spend isn't yet earning its keep. Productivity value unlocked = the additional people we help reach "using AI well" (we model training lifting adoption toward ~75%) × hours saved/week × 52 weeks × loaded hourly cost. LearningGPT cost = team size × the annual per-seat price you selected. We deliberately ignore harder-to-measure gains (quality, morale, retention, faster onboarding) and count only time saved. Change any number to your own — this is a model to think with, not a promise.
