Your very first chat with AI — nothing to be afraid of.

If you can send a text message, you can use AI. It's just a friendly helper you talk to by typing. Let's take the very first step together — slowly, and with nothing to worry about.

The idea

AI is a helper you talk to in plain words.

Think of it like a patient assistant who has read a great deal and is happy to help. You type a question or a request, and it writes an answer back, the same way a knowledgeable friend might. You don't need any special words or buttons — just type the way you'd speak.

Remember

You truly cannot break it. There is no wrong question, and nothing bad happens if you make a typo or ask something simple. Take your time.

Step 1Open a free AI helper

The most common one is called ChatGPT, and there's a free version. You can open it in your web browser at chatgpt.com, or our own playground right here. You'll see a box at the bottom that says something like 'Message' or 'Ask anything.'

Step 2Type a question, just like talking

Click in that box and type a real question in your own words. Then press Enter (or tap the arrow). In a few seconds, an answer appears. That's it — that's a 'chat.'

Try typing thisHello! I'm new to this. Can you explain what you can help me with, in simple terms?

Step 3Keep the conversation going

You don't start over each time. You can reply to what it said, just like a back-and-forth conversation. If an answer is confusing, ask it to explain more simply.

If something's unclear, typeThat was a little confusing. Can you explain it again, more simply, like I'm brand new to this?
Good to know: AI can sometimes get things wrong — it's confident even when it shouldn't be. For anything important (health, money, legal), always double-check with a real person you trust. We'll cover staying safe in the very next lesson.

Your first try

Let's get one real chat under your belt. There's no wrong way to do this:

  1. Open a free AI helper.
  2. Type: 'Tell me one interesting fact about [something you like].'
  3. Read the answer, then type 'Tell me more.' and watch it continue.
  4. That's it — you've used AI.
