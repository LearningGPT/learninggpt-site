Deep Research: analyst output, on demand.

Deep Research is Perplexity's long-running mode — upgraded to run on Claude Opus 4.5 for Max and Pro users. Unlike a chat answer, it doesn't just write back text: it produces presentations, spreadsheets, dashboards, and websites as finished deliverables. The skill is asking for the right format, then verifying the numbers before you ship.

The mental model

It builds the deliverable, not just the answer.

A normal search hands you text. Deep Research reads across 20+ sources and produces the artifact your task actually needs — a deck for the board, a comparison sheet for procurement, a dashboard stakeholders can poke at themselves.

One-shot deep dive → a finished report, deck, sheet, or dashboard. For "I need the deliverable now."

Why it matters

AI tools report outdated or wrong numbers with total confidence. Funding figures, customer logos, and pricing are exactly where Deep Research drifts — and exactly what a reader will challenge. The deliverable is 80% there, not 100%; the last 20% is your verification and your voice.

Challenge: order a deliverable instead of a search

Pick a task where you'd normally build a slide deck or a spreadsheet by hand. Have Deep Research produce the deliverable, specifying the exact format and structure. Spot-check the numbers, polish for your voice and brand, then compare to your manual workflow — time, source quality, and design effort.

What you can do now

  • Ask for a specific output format — deck, sheet, dashboard — not just text
  • Spell out the structure (slides, columns, filters) in the prompt
  • Spot-check funding, customer, and pricing claims against the source
  • Treat output as 80% done — polish for voice, brand, and accuracy
  • Use Deep Research for one-shot deliverables, a Space for ongoing work
