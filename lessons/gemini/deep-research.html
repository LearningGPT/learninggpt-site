Deep Research: Gemini does your homework.

Gemini's Deep Research mode lets it spend 5-15 minutes doing real multi-source research before answering. It's the closest thing to having a research analyst on staff. This lesson covers when Deep Research wins, how to prompt it for best results, and how to evaluate the output before trusting it.

The mental model

Deep Research is for questions you'd otherwise hire someone to research.

Don't use it for quick facts (regular search is faster) or creative work (it's overkill). Use it for the questions where you need 10-30 sources synthesized, cited, and structured into a real report.

Workflow 01 Prompt Deep Research for real depth

1

Specific question, broad search

Vague prompts produce vague reports. Give Deep Research a specific, structured question.

The prompt that works

Good Deep Research promptsResearch the AI training market for enterprises. Include: market size, top 5 players, pricing models, recent funding, regulatory landscape, predicted growth. Research the impact of [recent regulation] on the [industry]. Include affected companies, compliance timelines, and how leading firms are responding.

Best use cases

  • Industry/market analyses
  • Competitive landscapes
  • Regulatory or policy research
  • Pre-decision due diligence on major topics
Deep Research takes 5-15 minutes per run. Don't kick it off and walk away expecting instant results.
Time savings: Hours of analyst-time research: 15 minutes of Gemini time + review.

Workflow 02 Refine the research plan before running

2

Use the plan-first checkpoint

When Deep Research starts, it shows you its research plan — what it will look at, what sub-questions it will answer. Don't accept the default; refine it.

The prompt that works

Plan refinement(After Deep Research proposes a plan) Add a section on [aspect you care about]. Drop the section on [aspect that's not relevant]. Focus more on [specific angle].

Best use cases

  • Customizing reports for your specific decision context
  • Excluding irrelevant tangents
  • Ensuring the report answers YOUR question, not a generic one
If the plan doesn't include something obviously important, that's a signal Deep Research won't surface it. Add it manually before kicking off.
Time savings: Lower-quality output from generic plans: avoided.

Workflow 03 Evaluate the output before trusting

3

Three checks every Deep Research report needs

Don't ship Deep Research output as-is. Run three quick checks first.

The prompt that works

Three checks(1) Sources: are they credible? Click through 3-5 random citations. (2) Recency: are date-sensitive claims actually current? (3) Coverage: did it miss anything you know matters? Add notes manually.

Best use cases

  • Pre-publication or pre-meeting verification
  • Building trust calibration with Deep Research over time
  • Catching hallucinated or low-quality sources
Deep Research generally cites well but occasionally pulls from SEO-spam sources. The 3-source check protects you.
Time savings: Trust-but-verify becomes a 2-min ritual.

Final challenge: one real Deep Research run

Pick a real question you've been meaning to research — for a meeting, a decision, a strategy doc. Use Deep Research. Refine the plan. Evaluate the output with the three checks. Compare to what you'd have produced manually.
