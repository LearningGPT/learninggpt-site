01 · Named agents aren't "smarter AI" — they're specialized

The first generation of AI was generic: one model, and you bend it to your task with careful prompting. The second generation is named agents — AI workers pre-tuned for a specific job and wired into your M365 data.

Microsoft's bet: most knowledge work doesn't need one all-powerful AI. It needs a researcher, an analyst, a facilitator, a sales rep, a service agent — each tuned for its role and working alongside your team.

The practical win: zero prompt engineering. You don't coax a generic model into acting like a researcher — Researcher already is one. You pick the right coworker for the task instead of crafting the perfect prompt. The skill shifts from prompting to knowing who to ask.

02 · Your eight Copilot coworkers

What each does, and where it lives. You won't memorize this — the gym next will lock it in.

The analyst duo · in Copilot Chat
Researcher
Synthesizes your documents and the web into a structured brief.
Where: Copilot Chat · e.g. "Competitive analysis on Competitor X using our SharePoint notes + the web."
Analyst
Crunches your numbers and builds charts — your Excel files, CRM data, dashboards.
Where: Copilot Chat · e.g. "Analyze Q1 rep performance and build 3 charts for the exec deck."
Meetings · in Teams
Facilitator
Live notes, action items, and a decision log during a Teams meeting.
Where: Teams meetings · e.g. Ask mid-meeting "what did we decide on the budget?" without breaking flow.
Interpreter
Real-time speech-to-speech translation across languages.
Where: Teams meetings · e.g. You speak English, they hear French — both transcripts generated.
Revenue & support · in Dynamics 365 / Copilot
Sales Agent
Pre-call research, lead scoring, follow-up drafting, pipeline hygiene (with its Qualification & Development siblings).
Where: Dynamics 365 / Copilot · e.g. A Teams auto-brief 2 hours before each meeting with account history + smart questions.
Customer Service Agent
Ticket triage, responses drafted from your knowledge base, SLA tracking and auto-escalation.
Where: Dynamics 365 / Copilot · e.g. Incoming tickets categorized, prioritized, and drafted before an agent opens them.
Knowledge & custom
SharePoint Agent
Answers questions grounded only in a specific SharePoint site or library.
Where: SharePoint · e.g. Staff Q&A bot over your HR policy library — answers from your docs, nothing else.
Copilot Studio
The platform for building your own agent when the named ones don't fit your workflow.
Where: Copilot Studio · e.g. A custom expense-approval agent on your APIs, deployed into Copilot Chat.
