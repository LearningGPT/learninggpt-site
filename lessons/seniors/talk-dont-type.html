Talk, don't type — just speak, and AI listens.

If typing is slow or hard on your hands and eyes, here's wonderful news: you can simply talk to AI out loud, and it talks back. For many people, this is the moment AI finally clicks.

The idea

Speaking is often easier than typing — and it works beautifully.

Most AI helpers on a phone have a little microphone button. Tap it, speak naturally like you're talking to a person, and it hears you. Some can even read their answers back to you out loud. No more squinting or hunting for keys.

Try itTap the microphone and "speak"

Tap the big button below to see exactly how it feels — it listens, shows your words, and reads a short answer back. (This is a gentle preview; the real one uses your phone's microphone.)

Tap to speak
Good to know: Voice works best in a quiet room. If it mishears you, that's normal — just repeat yourself or speak a little slower. You're not doing anything wrong; it gets better as you go.

In the real appWhere to find it

In the AI app on your phone, look for a small microphone icon near the typing box (and sometimes a headphone or 'voice' icon for a full spoken conversation). If reading on screen tires you, ask it to keep answers short or read them aloud.

If you can't find it, ask (or say)I'm using [the app] on my [iPhone/Android phone]. Where is the microphone or voice button, and how do I talk to you out loud? Explain step by step.

Have one spoken chat

Give your eyes and hands a rest:

  1. Open the AI app on your phone.
  2. Find and tap the microphone button.
  3. Ask a question out loud, naturally.
  4. Notice how much easier that felt — and use it from now on.
