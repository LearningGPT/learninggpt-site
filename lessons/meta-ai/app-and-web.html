The standalone app: Meta AI, full strength.

The in-app helpers are convenient, but the standalone Meta AI app and website are where it is most capable. If you want it as a real daily assistant, this is the home base.

01Why the standalone app is better

It gives you the full assistant — longer conversations, richer responses, image generation, document and image input, and a persistent history — rather than the lightweight in-chat version. It also keeps your AI conversations separate from your messaging.

02Voice

The app does natural voice conversations — talk to it hands-busy, ask follow-ups without repeating yourself, and get spoken answers. It is the closest thing to the glasses experience without the hardware.

The Discover feed

A feed of prompts and creations from others — useful for learning what is possible and finding prompt ideas you can remix. Treat it as inspiration, then make the prompts your own.

When to use which

In-chat Meta AI for quick conveniences where you already are; the standalone app/web for real work, voice, and longer sessions.
