Grok in 2026: everything it can do, explained.

Grok grew from "the chatbot inside X" into a full platform: frontier models, live-feed intelligence no competitor has, a research agent, image and video generation, voice, connectors into your tools, and an agent mode that works while you don't. Here is the whole map — and the honest read on each piece.

01The one unfair advantage

Every AI assistant can summarize an article. Only Grok sits on top of X's live feed — hundreds of millions of posts a day, searchable and analyzable in real time. Breaking news, market chatter, public sentiment, what people are saying about your industry right now: this is Grok's home turf, and the realtime-intelligence lesson turns it into a skill.

The honest framing

Real-time is the moat. For the rest — careful long-form writing, private document analysis, enterprise controls — Grok is competitive but not uniquely best. We teach when to reach for it and when not to; that judgment is the whole game.

02The capability map

Models & modes: fast everyday responses or deeper "think" reasoning on hard problems. DeepSearch: the research agent that reads dozens of sources and returns a cited brief. Imagine: image generation, and video with sound. Voice: natural spoken conversation with distinct personalities. Connectors & Canvas: hooks into files and tools, plus a workspace for building alongside the chat. Agent Mode: hand Grok a multi-step task and let it run. Each has a dedicated lesson on this track.

03The pricing ladder, decoded

Grok's pricing is a ladder: a limited free tier, access bundled with X Premium subscriptions, and dedicated SuperGrok plans topping out at a heavy-usage tier priced for professionals who lean on it all day. The honest rule: most people should start free or low, upgrade only when they hit real limits — and very few need the top rung. What Grok costs walks the ladder dollar by dollar.

Grok answers with confidence and personality — including when it is wrong. The same verification habits we teach everywhere apply doubly on a platform wired into social media.

04Where to start

Begin with Grok basics (free) to get hands-on, then follow the track in order: costs, DeepSearch, real-time intelligence, and on into images, video, voice, and the work tools. By the end you will know both halves — what Grok can do, and when it is the right tool for the job.
