Connect Claude to your Outlook inbox — and what to do once you have.

Most AI is only useful when it can see your data. Claude can't read your inbox by default — but one 10-minute setup gives it secure access to your Microsoft 365 email and calendar. After that it can triage, draft, find that one email, and brief you before a meeting. This lesson is the setup plus the five workflows worth doing once you're connected — and where Claude beats Outlook, and where it doesn't.

The setup is the lesson

Connect Claude to Microsoft 365 (~10 min)

  1. In Claude.ai → Settings → Connectors, find Microsoft 365 and click Connect.
  2. Sign in on the Microsoft window with your work email (e.g. dan@learninggpt.ai).
  3. Approve MFA on your phone. No MFA yet? Set it up at aka.ms/mfasetup first — Microsoft now requires it for API access.
  4. Review and accept the permissions (email, calendar, OneDrive, SharePoint, Teams).
  5. Back in Claude you'll see Connected. Test it: "Summarize my inbox from the last 24 hours."

Once you're connected

Five workflows worth the setup

① Daily triage
Group the last 24h into reply-this-week / FYI / archive — a 90-second read instead of a 25-min scan.
"Search my inbox from the last 24h. Group as: needs my reply this week, FYI only, safe to archive."
② Draft replies in your voice
Claude mirrors the sender's tone and drafts a sendable reply. It drafts; you send.
"Draft a reply to [name] agreeing in principle but pushing the deadline to Friday. Match their tone. Under 80 words."
③ Find that one email
Semantic search by meaning, not keywords — the thread you can describe but can't recall.
"Find the email where I quoted a discount to a client last quarter. What did I offer?"
④ Weekly review
A Friday recap you'd never do by hand: open threads, commitments, replies you owe, patterns.
"Summarize my week: key threads + where they stand, commitments to follow up, replies I owe, patterns worth noticing."
⑤ Pre-meeting prep
Five minutes before a meeting, a one-paragraph brief from everything relevant in your inbox.
"I meet [name] in 15 min. Surface anything they've sent recently and any open items between us. Brief me."

Privacy & what it still can't do

Connecting an AI to your inbox feels invasive the first time. Here's what's actually happening, and where the boundaries are.

Good to know

  • Claude.ai chats aren't used for model training by default (Anthropic's published policy).
  • Microsoft processes the requests — your IT admin's logs reflect Claude's queries.
  • You stay in control — revoke access anytime in your Microsoft account settings.
  • Still use judgment — don't put regulated data (PHI, PII, financials) in prompts unless cleared.

Honest limitations

  • Can't send email — by design. Claude drafts; you send.
  • Archived folders are inconsistent — recent inbox works best.
  • Native Outlook is faster for simple exact lookups.
  • Shared mailboxes need specific admin configuration.
  • MFA can break the connection periodically — reconnect, it's a 30-second fix.
