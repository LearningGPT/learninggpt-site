AI for security — spot threats & respond faster.

You've used AI safely and set the rules. Now flip it around: turn AI into a security ally that reviews for risks, makes sense of alerts, and helps you respond to threats faster — without handing it the keys.

The mental model

AI is a tireless junior analyst: fast at triage and explanation, but it needs your judgment on the call.

AI won't replace security expertise, but it dramatically speeds the grunt work — reading a confusing alert, spotting risky patterns, drafting a response plan. You stay the decision-maker; AI does the first pass so you act faster on what matters.

The reframeAI explains and drafts; you decide and act. Let it turn an intimidating log or alert into plain English — then verify before you act on anything that blocks, deletes, or notifies.

Triage promptExplain this alert/log in plain English: what likely happened, how serious, and what I should check to confirm. Suggest a response, but flag what I must verify myself before acting. Don't assume — tell me what's uncertain.

Step 02 · Use AI as an ally — safely

AI can review a config, a contract, or a phishing email for risks, and draft an incident response plan. Tap each use — sound, or a step too far?

AI accelerates security; it doesn't own it. Never paste live secrets or credentials into it, never let it take destructive actions unattended, and always verify its read before you act — a confident wrong diagnosis can cause the very incident you're trying to prevent. It's a force multiplier for your judgment, not a replacement for it.
