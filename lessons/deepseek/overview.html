DeepSeek explained: frontier reasoning, rock-bottom price.

DeepSeek is the open-weight lab that made everyone else cut prices. It delivers near-frontier reasoning and coding at roughly a tenth of the cost of the big US models — through a free chat app and a drop-in API. Here is what it is, why it matters, and how to start.

01What DeepSeek actually is

DeepSeek is a Chinese AI lab whose open-weight models combine strong reasoning with prices far below the frontier. You can use it three ways: the free chat app and website, a paid API that mirrors OpenAI and Anthropic's formats, or by running the open weights yourself. The headline is value — capability that lands close to the top models, at a cost that changes what is affordable.

02The lineup: V4 Flash and V4 Pro

Two models cover most needs. V4 Flash is fast and extremely cheap — the everyday workhorse. V4 Pro trades speed for deeper reasoning on hard problems. Both share a very large ~1M-token context window, so they can read entire documents or codebases in one go.

Why it broke the cost curve

DeepSeek showed you could train and serve a near-frontier model for a fraction of the assumed cost — then published the weights. That combination of price and openness forced competitors to rethink pricing and put serious AI within reach of solo builders and small teams.

03Where it fits — and where it doesn't

Reach for DeepSeek when cost or openness matters: high-volume tasks, self-hosting, reasoning and coding on a budget. It is less of a fit when you need the absolute strongest model on a frontier task, the deepest multimodal features, or a vendor with Western enterprise compliance guarantees. For most everyday work, the gap is small and the savings are not.

Frequently asked

DeepSeek — your questions, answered

What is DeepSeek?
DeepSeek is an open-weight AI lab whose models offer near-frontier reasoning and coding at a fraction of the price of leading US models. You can use it through a free chat app, a paid OpenAI-compatible API, or by running the open weights yourself.
Is DeepSeek free?
Yes — the chat app and website are free to use. The API is paid but very cheap, and typically includes a free starter grant of tokens. Running the open weights yourself is free software; you pay only for your own compute.
What are V4 Flash and V4 Pro?
V4 Flash is the fast, cheap everyday model; V4 Pro is slower but reasons more deeply on hard problems. Both share roughly a 1M-token context window.
Is DeepSeek safe to use for work?
It is capable and widely used, but it is a Chinese lab, so apply the same judgment you would with any AI: keep regulated or confidential data out unless your organization has cleared it. For sensitive workloads, the open weights can be self-hosted.
