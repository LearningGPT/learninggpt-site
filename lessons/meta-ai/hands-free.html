Hands-free: run your day without your phone.

The point of AI glasses is keeping your hands and attention free. Here are the workflows that genuinely replace pulling out your phone a hundred times a day.

Messaging

Hear incoming messages, ask for a summary of a group, and reply by voice across WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, and your phone — or by handwriting with the Neural Band when you need to be quiet. Stay connected without stopping what you are doing.

Music & audio

Control playback across services — Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music — by voice, in multiple languages. Skip, pause, and switch without touching your phone, mid-walk or mid-task.

Reminders & lookups

Set reminders, ask quick factual questions, get directions, and capture a thought the instant it arrives — "Hey Meta, remind me to email Sam at 3." The friction of unlocking a phone is what kills these intentions; glasses remove it.

The real benefit

It is not any single feature — it is removing the phone-unlock tax from dozens of micro-actions a day, so small intentions actually happen.
