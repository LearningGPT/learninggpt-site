Writing that still sounds like you.

Writing is ChatGPT's most-used job and its most misused one. The difference between generic AI slop and fast, good writing that sounds like you is a set of workflows — here they are.

01 The zero draft

The blank page is the most expensive part of writing. Kill it: dump everything you know as messy notes — fragments, bullets, voice-memo transcript, whatever — and ask ChatGPT to "turn this into a rough first draft; keep my phrasings where they're good." That last clause matters: it seeds the draft with your actual voice instead of the model's defaults.

02 Rewrite surgically, not wholesale

"Make it better" produces beige. Name the single dimension you want moved:

Instead ofSay
"Improve this""Cut 40% without losing the warning in paragraph two"
"Make it professional""Same content, but confident instead of apologetic"
"Punch it up""Give me three alternative opening lines, each with a different energy"
"Fix the tone""Reader is annoyed and skimming. Rewrite so the key ask survives a 10-second read"

03 Teach it your voice once

Collect three to five things you've written that sound like you on a good day. Paste them in and ask: "Describe this writer's voice as a style guide — rhythm, vocabulary, what they never do." Save that style guide in your custom instructions or a writing Project. From then on, "write it in my voice" means something.

The slop test

Before sending anything ChatGPT helped write, scan for its tells: "delve," "furthermore," "it's important to note," rule-of-three sentences everywhere, em-dash confetti, a suspiciously balanced closing paragraph. If you spot two or more, one instruction fixes it: "Rewrite plainly. Short sentences. No filler phrases."

04 The review pass you're not using

ChatGPT is a better editor than writer. Before anything important goes out, run: "Read this as [the actual recipient]. What lands badly? What's unclear? What question will they immediately ask?" This catches the thing you can't see — how it reads to someone who isn't you. For high-stakes messages, also ask: "What's the least charitable reading of this?"

05 Where to draw the line

Some writing is the relationship: condolences, real apologies, the note that only counts if it cost you something. Draft those yourself. Use the machine to check clarity if you want — but the words should be yours. Knowing where this line sits is part of being good at this.
