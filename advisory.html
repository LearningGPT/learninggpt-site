Straight answers

The questions everyone asks.

Do you take money from the AI companies?

Never. No affiliate fees, referral commissions, rev-share, or partner deals — from any AI vendor. You're the only one who pays us, which is the whole point. If we ever recommend a tool, it's because it's right for you, full stop.

Do you sell or resell software and licenses?

No. We're strictly advisory. We tell you what fits and you buy it direct from whoever provides it — or from no one. We never touch the transaction, because the moment we did, we'd have a reason to steer you.

What if we've already picked a tool?

Great — we'll give you an honest second opinion. Sometimes the answer is "you chose well, keep it and here's how to get more out of it." Sometimes it's "here's a cheaper or better fit." Either way you'll know.

Do you implement it for us?

We advise and plan the direction; we don't build it or resell licenses. Keeping our hands off the implementation is exactly what keeps the advice neutral. We'll hand you a clear plan your team (or your existing IT partner) can execute.

Why should we trust your read?

We run LearningGPT — 235+ hands-on lessons across every major AI tool, kept current as they change. The same independent research behind the lessons is the research behind your recommendation. We've already done the homework on these tools; we're just applying it to your company.

How much time does it take from us?

Very little. A Snapshot is mostly async — a short questionnaire and one round of questions. An Assessment adds a few short interviews and a live readout, over 2–3 weeks. Fixed fee, agreed up front, no surprises.
