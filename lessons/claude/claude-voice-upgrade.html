Claude’s voice mode just grew up.

Until this week, talking to Claude meant talking to Haiku — fast, but built for quick answers, not hard thinking. On July 23, 2026, Anthropic changed that: voice mode now runs Opus and Sonnet too, reaches into your connected tools, and speaks eleven languages. The result is less “voice assistant,” more “thinking partner you can pace the room with.” Here’s what changed and how to use it.

The headline

Voice mode is now in beta for every chat user on mobile, desktop, and web. Pick your model mid-conversation, let Claude act in tools like Gmail, Slack, Google Calendar, and Canva (with permission), and switch between 11 languages. Free plan: Haiku + one tool. Paid: all models + all your tools.

01 Why the model swap matters

Anthropic originally put Haiku behind voice mode for one reason: speed. That kept conversations snappy — and shallow. People were already using voice sessions to work through real business problems out loud, and Haiku would keep up but not keep depth. Now the model picker works in voice: choose Opus or Sonnet for hard problems, and Claude uses the fastest version of whichever model you pick so the conversation still flows. It defaults to the last model you used in text chat, so switching between typing and talking doesn’t reset anything.

One design choice worth knowing: Claude’s voice mode takes turns. It listens, pauses to think, then responds. It feels different from assistants that fire back instantly — that pause is the more capable model actually working.

02 What a “thinking out loud” session looks like

Rehearse
Practice a pitch or a hard conversation out loud and ask Claude for feedback on your reasoning and your delivery.
Decide
Talking through two competing offers forces you to say what you actually value — Claude asks the follow-up questions instead of handing you an answer.
Poke holes
Walk through a client proposal and ask Claude to find the gaps in your logic before your client does.
Brainstorm
Roadmap ideas, video concepts, why the last one worked — messy, spoken, half-formed thinking is exactly what this is for.

03 From talking to doing

The bigger shift: a voice conversation can now end in an action. Once you’ve decided something out loud, ask Claude to do it in your connected tools:

  • Running late? “Push my 2pm on Google Calendar back 30 minutes.”
  • Finished talking through the pitch? “Turn that into a one-pager in Canva.”
  • Walking to the office? “Summarize today’s email and draft replies to the critical ones.”

Claude asks permission before touching any connected tool. You connect tools in Settings → Connectors on mobile, desktop, or web.

An AI acting in your email and calendar from a spoken instruction is powerful and worth a beat of caution: keep the confirmation prompts on, and give Claude access only to tools you actually want it acting in. Our Secure Your AI Use track covers the habit set.

04 Eleven languages, one catch

Voice mode now speaks English, French, German, Hindi, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Brazilian Portuguese, and Spanish in both Latin American and European variants — on every plan, including Free. You can switch mid-conversation.

Claude does not auto-detect your language. Ask out loud (“switch to Spanish”) or set it in the voice settings language picker — and note that voice language is set separately, so your existing language settings don’t carry over.

05 Free vs. paid, and the fine print

  • Free plan: Haiku in voice, one connected tool, all languages.
  • Paid plans: Opus and Sonnet in voice, plus all your connected tools.
  • Limits: voice conversations count toward your regular usage limits — a long Opus voice session spends the same budget as heavy Opus text use.
  • Where: beta on mobile, desktop, and web; Anthropic says it works best from your phone. Tap the sound-wave icon in the mobile app to start.
Try this today

Take one decision you’ve been chewing on, go for a ten-minute walk, and talk it through with Sonnet in voice mode. End with: “Summarize what I actually said I wanted.” That summary — your own reasoning, played back — is the feature’s best trick.

Frequently asked

Claude voice mode, quick answers

Is the upgrade free?
Partly. Every plan gets voice mode in beta with all languages. Free gets Haiku plus one connected tool; paid plans unlock Opus, Sonnet, and all your connected tools. Voice counts toward your normal usage limits.
Can I change models mid-conversation?
Yes — use the model picker without ending the session. Voice uses the fastest version of whichever model you choose, and defaults to the last model you used in text.
Will Claude act in my apps without asking?
No — Claude asks permission before using a connected tool. You control which tools are connected at all in Settings → Connectors.
Does it auto-detect my language?
No. Ask out loud to switch, or pick your language in voice settings — it’s a separate setting from your general language preferences.
