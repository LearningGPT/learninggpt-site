V8.2 is the new default: taste, learned.

On July 24, 2026, Midjourney flipped the switch: V8.2 is now the default model for every prompt you run. No flags, no settings, no extra cost. It's a focused update — aesthetics, consistency, and a personalization system that finally rewards all those image ratings you've been clicking. Here's the whole briefing, and the one thing worth doing this week.

What Midjourney says changed:

  • Bolder default output. Images should come out "more creative, bold, sophisticated, edgy and fresh" — Midjourney's words. In practice: V8.2 takes bigger aesthetic swings by default.
  • A higher floor. The "random situations where you'd get a low quality image" — the one dud in a grid of four — "should be dramatically reduced." For working professionals this is the bigger deal: fewer rerolls, more usable grids.
  • Personalization that knows you. Profiles now "understand" your taste much better, especially if you have a large pile of ratings behind them. And building a fresh profile for V8.2 draws from a much larger, improved pool of images than before.

The backstory, briefly: V8.2 spent a month in public testing behind the --preview flag (since June 25) before being promoted to default. If you've been prompting with --preview, you can drop it — you're on V8.2 either way now. Everyone else moved over automatically on July 24.

What to actually do: Midjourney "strongly encourages" trying both your old personalization profiles and a newly created one against V8.2 — the model reads them differently, and the new profile-building pool is better. Run the same prompt three ways: no profile, old profile, fresh profile. Ten minutes, and you'll know which version of your taste V8.2 understands best.

If personalization profiles are new to you, our style references lesson covers how Midjourney learns and applies your aesthetic — the mechanics haven't changed, just how well they work.

What this doesn't change: your plan, your pricing, your parameters. Prompting fundamentals from the prompt anatomy lesson carry straight over. And as with every model bump: your favorite old prompts will render differently. That's not a bug — rerun the ones you care about and re-save the winners.

Frequently asked

Midjourney V8.2, quick answers

Do I need to switch anything on?
No. V8.2 became the default on July 24, 2026. Every prompt uses it automatically; --preview is no longer needed.
Should I rebuild my personalization profile?
Test before you rebuild. Old profiles work — and often work better under V8.2 — but new profiles draw from an improved image pool. Try both against the same prompt and keep the winner.
Does it cost more?
No — same plans, same pricing. V8.2 simply replaces V8.1 as the standard model.
Will my old prompts look the same?
Not exactly — V8.2 is bolder by default and the aesthetic shifted. Rerun important prompts and re-save the versions you like under the new model.
