Privacy on Meta AI: what it sees, what to lock down.

Meta AI's strength — being everywhere — is also its privacy question. Here is a clear, non-alarmist look at what it can access and the settings worth changing.

01What it can touch

Your conversations with Meta AI, the content you share with it (photos, questions, messages you ask it to summarize or draft), and on glasses, what the camera and mic capture when active. It operates inside Meta's ecosystem, so treat it accordingly.

02Settings worth changing

  1. Review AI data and history controls — delete past chats and limit what is retained.
  2. Check what Meta AI is allowed to access (camera, mic, contacts) and trim it.
  3. On glasses, understand the capture indicator and what syncs to the app.
  4. Decide what you will never put through Meta AI — regulated, confidential, or others' private info.
A sensible posture

Use Meta AI freely for low-stakes convenience. For anything confidential, regulated, or about other people, keep it out — and on glasses, be transparent with people around you. Default to the most private settings and loosen only as needed.

Do not paste sensitive personal, financial, or work-confidential data into any AI — including Meta AI — unless your organization has explicitly cleared it.

Review and limit AI data and history, trim its access to camera, mic, and contacts, understand the glasses capture indicator, and keep sensitive data out.

Meta uses interactions to improve its services per its policies, so review your AI data controls and avoid sharing anything sensitive.
