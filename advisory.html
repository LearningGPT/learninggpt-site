How we stay honest

The independence pledge.

The people selling you the tool cannot also be the ones grading it. Everything below is a commitment, not a positioning statement.

  • We take no money from AI vendors

    No affiliate fees, referral commissions, rev‑share, or “partner” kickbacks. Not now, not ever.

  • We don't sell software or licenses

    Strictly advisory. You buy direct from whoever fits, or from nobody at all. We just point the way.

  • You pay us, and only you

    A fixed fee for honest judgment — never a cut of whatever you end up buying.

  • No exclusive vendor relationships

    So we can name the right tool for you, and tell you exactly where it falls short.

  • Sometimes the answer is “spend less”

    If the free tier, a cheaper tool, or the one you already own is the right call, that's the call you'll get. The independence that makes our training trustworthy is the same thing that makes the advice worth paying for — we won't trade it for a commission.
